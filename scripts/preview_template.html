My take

I built this simulation of a $120M site infrastructure scope to show how I would work as a cost engineer. The numbers are simulated; the thinking is mine. Each study starts with a question a project manager would ask, goes to the data, and ends with a dollar impact and a recommendation, because a cost report should lead to a decision, not just a number.
